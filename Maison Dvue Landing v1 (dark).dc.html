Chapter I — The Philosophy

Selection over addition.
Affinity over ornament.

Conventional beauty treats hair and skin as separate territories. Skincare is named, traceable, proportioned with care. Haircare is blended — rarely with the same ceremony.

We began at the other end. Fourteen rare botanical essences, chosen for what they are and what they do, held in an anhydrous composition — no water, no filler, nothing to dilute the intent.

A composition made with care earns the right to be used freely.

Chapter III — The Ritual

Five drops. Three motions.

Worn on damp lengths after washing, or dry through the day. Morning, evening, or both — the composition asks nothing of your schedule.

I
Five drops

Into the palm. The dropper meters the composition so you never over-give.

II
Warm between palms

Body heat opens the essences. Sandalwood arrives first, rose lifts behind it.

III
Draw through

Mid-length to ends, then whatever remains over the surface. Nothing to rinse.
