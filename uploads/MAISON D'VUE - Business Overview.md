# MAISON D'VUE — Business Overview

*Compiled from maisondvue.com, shop.maisondvue.com, and SlaqAI ClickUp client records. Last updated: August 11, 2026.*

## Brand Identity

MAISON D'VUE is an American luxury haircare house, founded in Beverly Hills in 2026. The brand describes itself as "The House of Affinity Compositions™" — its core proprietary concept. The name follows the old apostrophe-D naming convention (in the style of a French maison) and is pronounced "May-zon-day-VOO," signaling heritage and lineage without literal translation.

The brand's central idea, **Affinity Compositions™**, is defined as anhydrous botanical compositions engineered for biological affinity with the specific surface of the body that receives them (hair, skin, etc.). The positioning argument is that conventional beauty treats hair and skin as separate territories, formulated to very different standards — skincare with named, traceable, carefully-proportioned ingredients, haircare typically blended without the same ceremony. MAISON D'VUE's pitch is to bring skincare-level rigor and reverence to haircare first, then expand the same philosophy to other categories.

Brand philosophy, as stated on-site, rests on a few explicit principles: selection over addition, affinity over ornament, anhydrous formulation, minimal instruction ("a composition made with care earns the right to be used freely"), and a considered, unhurried tone of voice ("desire over disruption").

## Founder — Masiela Lusha

Masiela Lusha is Founder & Creative Director. Her biography is a major pillar of the brand's storytelling:

- **Background:** Raised across Tirana, Budapest, and Vienna before her family settled in Michigan; English was her fourth language.
- **Writing career:** Published poet since age 12 (*Inner Thoughts*, released simultaneously in two languages). Author of seven books total, including five poetry collections (*Drinking the Moon*, *Amore Celeste*, *The Call*, *The Living Air*, *The 12 Veils of Hope*), a novel (*The Besa*), two children's books, and translations of Mother Teresa's poems from Albanian. *The 12 Veils of Hope* was an Amazon best seller in modern poetry; its "movements" structure is cited as an influence on the brand's own architecture. She was named among North America's Top Ten Talented Poets and recognized by President Bill Clinton before graduating high school at 15, then admitted to UCLA as a junior at 18, graduating with a degree in English.
- **Acting career:** Best known for playing Carmen Lopez in the Warner Bros./ABC series *George Lopez* (five seasons, syndicated in 70+ countries, two Young Artist Awards). Other credits include *Blood: The Last Vampire* (Sony), the *Sharknado* franchise (SyFy), and voice work on PBS's *Clifford's Puppy Days*. Over 30 film/TV credits total.
- **Humanitarian work:** First Global Goodwill Ambassador of the World Assembly of Youth (a UN-affiliated youth body); first Ambassador for Sentebale (the charity founded by Prince Harry and Prince Seeiso of Lesotho); ambassador to Athgo International; founder of her own charity, Children of the World; active advocate for UN Women.
- **Personal:** Lives in Los Angeles with her husband, two children, and their dog, Hemingway.

The brand's narrative explicitly frames MAISON D'VUE as her "seventh book" — applying the same discipline of selection she uses in poetry to botanical formulation.

## The Product

### Vue 14™ Hair Elixir

The maison's first and currently only released product line is a hair elixir built around a patent-pending formulation system called the **VUE Affinity Matrix™**, composed of 14 rare, plant-derived botanical essences. The formula is free of silicones, sulfates, parabens, and mineral oils.

**Product formats and pricing:**
- **The Signature** — 30ml / 1 fl oz — **$125**
- **The Voyage** — 10ml / 0.33 fl oz (travel size) — **$68**

**Use ritual:** "Five drops. Three motions." — five drops into the palm, warmed between the palms until the essences open, then drawn through the hair. Positioned as usable wet (post-wash, on damp lengths) or dry (throughout the day as a finishing/frizz-taming touch), worn morning and/or evening.

**Signature scent:** Every current and future product will share one house scent, **Âme de Vue™** ("soul of Vue"), built around Australian Sandalwood (warmth/depth) and Bulgarian Rose (lift/light).

**Efficacy claims:** The brand is careful to position the elixir as *not* a hair-growth product. It claims to restore devitalized lengths, smooth the cuticle for shine, deliver weightless volume, and protect against breakage/length loss from friction, heat, color damage, and dryness — i.e., it creates conditions for hair to thrive rather than stimulating growth directly.

**Reviews:** 5.0 average, 9 reviews displayed on-site. Named reviewers include Brittany, Yasmine, Rita, and "T.S.," with several more unlabeled 5-star entries (Jessica, Ashley, Megan, Priya, Sarah).

## Go-to-Market Model

The brand sells via a limited-drop "**Allocation**" model rather than continuous inventory — monthly batches ("June Allocation," "July Allocation," "August Allocation") that sell out and reopen. As of the site content reviewed, June and July allocations were sold out, and the August allocation was open for reservation.

Per SlaqAI's ClickUp discovery-call notes: the product had **not yet formally launched** as of early August 2026. At that point, the brand had taken 50 pre-orders (subsequently refunded, pending stock) plus a 50-person waitlist generated from a single early influencer post. Full product launch was targeted for the first or second week of September 2026.

## Brand Architecture / Roadmap

The house is explicitly structured as a multi-category expansion, framed as "chapters" of the same book:

1. **Haircare** — Composed and released (Vue 14™ Hair Elixir).
2. **Makeup** — Forthcoming / in formulation.
3. **Lifestyle** — Forthcoming.

Each future category is described as entering "the same way: by composition, by affinity, by the same hand" — i.e., using the same Affinity Compositions™ methodology and the same Âme de Vue™ signature scent thread.
